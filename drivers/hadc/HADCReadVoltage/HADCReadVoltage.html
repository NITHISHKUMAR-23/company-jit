1. Description

This example demonstrates how to use HADC driver to convert and read the voltage levels via House-keeping ADC in the processor. This example is supported for EV-SOMCRR-EZKIT.

2. Processor

  • ADSP-21593
  • ADSP-SC594
  • ADSP-SC598

    • 3. Overview

    This example demonstrates how to use HADC driver to convert and read the voltage levels.
    four channels are used for ADSP-21593 and eight channels for ADSP-SC594, ADSP-SC598 in this example

    -- Channel VIN0 samples the VDD_INT voltage at ~1V.
    -- Channel VIN1 samples the VDD_A voltage at ~1.8V.
    -- Channel VIN2 samples the VDD_DMC voltage at ~1.35V.
    -- Channel VIN3 samples the VDD_INT voltage at ~0.9V.
    -- Channel VIN4 samples the VDD_VREF voltage at ~1.8V.
    -- Channel VIN5 samples the VDD_INT voltage at ~1V.
    -- Channel VIN6 samples the GND voltage at ~0V.
    -- Channel VIN7 samples the GND voltage at ~0V.

    After reading the voltage levels, the example also checks whether those are in expected tolerance range (+-0.1V). For each of the inputs, "Correct voltage" is printed if the voltage is within the range, and "Incorrect voltage" is printed otherwise.

    4. User Configuration Macros

    • PERFORM_BLOCKINGMODE (ReadVoltage.h)
      - When this MACRO is configured as (1), then the HADC driver uses blocking mode API to read ADC data.
    • PERFORM_NONBLOCKINGMODE (ReadVoltage.h)
      - When this MACRO is configured as (1), then the HADC driver uses non-blocking mode API to read ADC data.

    5. Hardware Setup

    The example requires that jumpers are placed across pin 1 & 2, 3 & 4, 5 & 6 and 7 & 8 of the P4 jumper for ADSP-21593.
    The example requires that jumpers are placed across pin 1 & 2, 3 & 4, 5 & 6 and 7 & 8 of the P4 and P5 jumpers for ADSP-SC594.
